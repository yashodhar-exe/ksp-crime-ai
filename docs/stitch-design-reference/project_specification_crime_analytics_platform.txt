# Karnataka State Police AI Crime Analytics & Investigation Platform - PRD

## Overview
A high-fidelity investigative suite for law enforcement professionals to analyze crime patterns, manage cases, and perform network analysis.

## Screen List & Flow
1. **Login Page**: Secure entry with Karnataka Police branding.
2. **Dashboard**: High-level KPIs, AI alerts, and district-wide trends.
3. **Cases Management**: Advanced filtering and search for FIR records.
4. **Case Details**: Deep dive into victims, suspects, timeline, and evidence.
5. **Citizens Module**: 360-degree view of individuals and their criminal relationships.
6. **Officer Management**: Performance tracking and case assignments.
7. **Search Intelligence**: Global unified search across multiple identifiers (Aadhaar, Phone, etc.).
8. **Criminal Network Analysis**: Visual graph of relationships and risk scores.
9. **Analytics Dashboard**: Geospatial heatmaps and predictive crime modeling.
10. **AI Investigation Assistant**: Conversational interface for case insights.
11. **Evidence Management**: Chain of custody and digital asset repository.
12. **Timeline View**: Chronological investigation milestones.
13. **User Management**: RBAC and audit logs for the department.
14. **Settings**: Platform configuration and security.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Decoration**: Focus on data density and legibility.
- **AI-First**: Dedicated space for AI summaries and suggestions.
- **Auditability**: Clear timestamps and officer attribution.
- **Scalability**: Responsive grid system for desktop workstations.
